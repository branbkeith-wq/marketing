Case Study — Cutting CAC 15%
Situation
Account structure had accumulated years of drift — broad match terms competing against themselves, bid strategy set up for an earlier stage of the account, and no regular pass to clear out non-converting search terms. CAC was trending up without a clear single cause.
Approach
Rebuilt the account in three moves: restructured match types so campaigns stopped cannibalizing each other's traffic, switched the bid strategy type to one better matched to current volume and conversion data, and ran a full negative keyword pass to cut spend on terms that looked relevant but never converted.
Result
CAC down 15%, with conversion rate held flat or improved — the savings came from efficiency, not from pulling back on volume.
Case Study — SEM Pacing Toolkit
Situation
Daily budget pacing spans Search, PMax, Shopping, and Video across both US and international markets. Checking it meant pulling numbers by hand, channel by channel, market by market, every morning — slow, and it meant pacing problems were often caught a day or more after they started.
Approach
Built an automation that refreshes the day's pacing on its own each morning, then: populates individual dashboards per channel with performance detail, flags any campaign trending hot or cold against QTD spend targets and conversion goals, tracks whether that trend has held for multiple consecutive days (so a one-day blip doesn't trigger a false alarm, but a real drift gets caught fast), keeps a change-log calendar of what changed and what it might affect, and sends a Slack briefing each morning with the day's follow-up priorities.
Result
6–10 hours a week that used to go to manual pacing checks, now available for actual optimization work — and pacing issues get caught in a morning instead of days later.
