Case Study — Lead Gen Rebuild: +40% MQLs on Lower Spend
Situation
Lead gen campaigns had accumulated years of drift — match types competing against each other, a bid strategy set up for an earlier stage of the account, and no regular pass to clear non-converting search terms. Cost per MQL was trending up without a single clear cause.
Approach
Rebuilt keyword and bid strategy in three moves: restructured match types so campaigns stopped cannibalizing each other's traffic, moved bidding to a strategy better matched to current volume and conversion data, and ran a full negative keyword pass to cut spend on terms that looked relevant but never converted.
Result
MQLs up 40% and cpMQL down 15% on 12% lower spend, quarter over quarter — the gains came from efficiency, not from pulling back on volume.

Case Study — SEM Pacing Toolkit (SA360 + AI-built automation)
Situation
Daily budget pacing spans Search, PMax, Shopping, and Video across US and international markets. Checking it meant pulling numbers by hand, channel by channel, market by market, every morning — slow, and pacing problems were often caught a day or more after they started.
Approach
Built an automation, integrated with SA360, that refreshes the day's pacing on its own each morning, then: populates individual dashboards per channel with performance detail, flags any campaign trending hot or cold against QTD spend targets and conversion goals, tracks whether that trend has held for multiple consecutive days (so a one-day blip doesn't trigger a false alarm, but a real drift gets caught fast), keeps a change-log calendar of what changed and what it might affect, and sends a Slack briefing each morning with the day's follow-up priorities. Built with AI-assisted development (Claude Code, Cursor).
Result
About 10 hours a week that used to go to manual pacing checks, now available for optimization work — and pacing issues get caught in a morning instead of days later.
